🎯 Operator-Level Content Strategy New
Different LLMs favor different page types — optimize your content specifically for how each model uses your TrustRadius pages
Why this matters: Each LLM has different content retrieval patterns. Optimizing for "AI" in general misses the signal — you need per-model GEO strategies. OpenAI and Perplexity alone account for 80% of your grounding requests.
OpenAI (ChatGPT) Top page type: Comparison (75% of their requests)
Profile: Comparison-heavy — buyers asking ChatGPT are in final evaluation mode, side-by-side feature comparisons
Content Actions
→ Ensure all comparison pages have structured feature tables (ChatGPT parses these well)
→ Add explicit "Why Zoom beats Teams/Meet" summary blocks — models prefer direct answers
→ Include pricing callouts in comparison page headers — ChatGPT buyers are price-sensitive at this stage
Anthropic (Claude) Top page type: Full Reviews (48% of their requests)
Profile: Review-heavy — Claude users tend to ask for in-depth, balanced product evaluations before deciding
Content Actions
→ Prioritize long-form, nuanced review content — Claude synthesizes depth better than brevity
→ Include "Ideal for / Not ideal for" sections — helps Claude give nuanced recommendations
→ Add third-party customer quotes to reviews — Claude weights social proof heavily in answers
Perplexity Top page type: Pricing (22% of their requests)
Profile: Pricing-disproportionate — Perplexity users ask "how much does X cost" more than any other LLM
Content Actions
→ Add transparent, scannable pricing tables — Perplexity excels at pulling structured price data
→ Include "price per seat at different tiers" tables on pricing page
→ Add a pricing FAQ section: "Is Zoom cheaper than Teams?" — Perplexity loves direct Q&A format
Google (Gemini) Top page type: Product Pages (48% of their requests)
Profile: Product-feature focused — Gemini pulls feature lists and integration specs for users asking about capabilities
Content Actions
→ Deep Google Workspace integration content performs best with Gemini
→ Feature comparison matrices with checkmark tables are highly retrievable
→ Include use-case sections by role (IT Admin, End User, Executive) — Gemini organizes answers by persona
